Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ReqBridge Local API handles information when you visit our website or use our VS Code extension. We follow strict data-minimization and local-first principles.

1. Local-First Processing & BYOK Keys

  • Your Postman collections, Thunder Client files, Bruno files, .http suites, OpenAPI specs, environment variables, and API responses are processed locally on your machine. We do not operate a cloud storage backend for your API workspaces.
  • If you use optional Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) features to translate complex pm.test() scripts, your API credentials are stored locally in vscode.SecretStorage and transmitted solely to the AI provider endpoint you configure.
  • This static website uses no third-party advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or behavioral profiling cookies.

2. License Activation & Payment Processing

  • Offline Ed25519 Keys: Signed RB1. license keys are verified locally in-process with zero network requests.
  • Merchant of Record (Global): Payments, invoicing, and tax compliance for global orders are processed by Creem (Armitage Labs OÜ) or Gumroad. We never receive or store your full payment card number or CVV.
  • Domestic Payment Channel (Mainland China): Optional RMB orders are processed by Mianbaoduo (mbd.pub) under their payment privacy terms.
  • Online License Validation: Activating or validating an online Creem/Gumroad key transmits only your license key and an anonymized device identifier (vscode.env.machineId) to enforce device seat limits.

3. Support Communications & Data Deletion

When you email soulkey529@gmail.com, we use your email address and order reference solely to resolve your support or refund request. To request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence (GDPR / CCPA), contact soulkey529@gmail.com.
